## 🌌 Overview
Recursive Quantum Dynamics (RQD) is a theoretical framework that derives General Relativity and Quantum Mechanics from a single underlying principle: **discrete recursion with finite memory.**

Instead of assuming a "background" spacetime, RQD posits that time and space emerge from the way information persists (mass) and flows (expansion).

$$\Lambda \approx \frac{1}{(c T_{age})^2}$$

This yields a value of **$\approx 5.9 \times 10^{-53} \text{ m}^{-2}$**, matching observations without the $10^{122}$ discrepancy found in Standard Quantum Field Theory.
