# Axioms and First Principles of RQD

This document outlines the formal derivation of the results presented in the main paper by Ashutosh Tiwari.

## Axiom 1: Recursive Emergence of Time
Time is not an absolute background. It is a bookkeeping parameter for discrete recursion steps $n$. The continuum limit $t = n\epsilon$ emerges as $\epsilon \to 0$.

## Axiom 2: Memory-Mass Equivalence
Mass ($m$) is the measure of "Memory Persistence." 
- **Inertia:** The resistance of a recursive state to dispersion.
- **Gravity:** The local "stiffening" of the recursion field caused by high memory density.

## Derivation of the $\Lambda$ Scale
We define $\Lambda$ as the residual curvature arising from recursion drift not bound into matter.

1. **The Maximum Scale ($L_{max}$):** In a causal recursive system, coherence cannot exceed the distance light has traveled since the initial state (Big Bang).
   $$L_{max} = c \times T_{age}$$

2. **The Residual Curvature:** Since curvature has units of $[Length]^{-2}$, and the only non-tuned scale in the global system is $L_{max}$:
   $$\Lambda_{predicted} = \frac{1}{L_{max}^2}$$

3. **Numerical Validation:**
   - $T_{age} \approx 4.35 \times 10^{17}$ s
   - $L_{max} \approx 1.3 \times 10^{26}$ m
   - **Result:** $\Lambda \approx 5.9 \times 10^{-53} \text{ m}^{-2}$ (Matches observed $\Lambda \approx 1.1 \times 10^{-52}$ within geometric factor $\alpha \approx 2$).
